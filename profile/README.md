<h1 align="center">Metablox</h1>

<p align="center">
  <strong>Travel memories, kept with the real places they happened.</strong>
</p>

<p align="center">
  Capture a moment on a trip and pin it to that place. City hubs collect<br />
  what people left there. A <strong>Blox</strong> is the place on the map — ownership<br />
  that keeps the spot and its stories together.
</p>
